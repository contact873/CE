The firm

Fifty-five years of standing behind the delivery.

Consolidated Engineers was established in 1969 and has done business with federal and provincial government departments ever since. The business model has changed over that time. The habit of accountability has not.

Introduction

A local agent with the manufacturer still on the line.

Our role is to be the reliable local principal for international manufacturers in Pakistan: the entity that carries the import, the documentation, the demonstration and the after-sales obligation.

Engagement with the defence department in 1985 established a position in the market that has held for four decades. Over the same period the business expanded into the civil departments of Pakistan, and was organised on modern business lines to become a supplier with the standing to take on contracts of any value.

Every new product is additionally indexed with the civilian departments and the Ministry of Defence before it is introduced to a user unit for demonstration and procurement. That is the work behind a supply contract, and we do it as a matter of course.

Organisation

Four functions, one contract.

A supply contract passes through four parts of the firm. The same person stays accountable across all four.

01

Principal relationships

Maintaining accredited agency agreements with foreign manufacturers, and the technical correspondence that goes with them.

02

Procurement and compliance

Indexing with the civilian departments and the Ministry of Defence, tender response, and the documentation a public contract requires.

03

Engineering and delivery

Specification against the site, import, installation, commissioning and calibration.

04

After-sales

Training, maintenance, repair and the continuing spares path for equipment already in service.

Vision

To excel in supporting the end user.

Our vision is to excel in supporting our end users and customers, with the support of the principals and manufacturers we represent.

That sentence is deliberately about the end user, not about us. The manufacturer supplies the equipment. We are accountable for the outcome.
