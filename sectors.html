Home / Sectors

Three sectors, one standard of delivery.

Public procurement and private procurement fail for different reasons. We structure the response to the sector, and keep the same accountability in both.

Sector 01

Civilian government departments

Federal and provincial departments requiring indexed products, transparent documentation and delivery against a published procurement timetable.

The work here is mostly preparation: getting the product indexed, matching the specification in the tender, and holding the delivery date once the contract is placed.

  • Product indexing and documentation
  • Tender and specification response
  • Delivery scheduling against the public timetable
  • Commissioning and operator training

Sector 02

Defence and military departments

Registered supply under the Directorate General Defence Purchase, Ministry of Defence Production, since 1985.

Defence procurement adds demonstration and user acceptance to the normal scope. We introduce the unit to the user formation, support the demonstration, and stay available for technical support after handover.

  • DGDP registration and Category A standing
  • Demonstration to the user unit
  • Documentation and import compliance
  • Technical support after handover
Discuss a requirement
Sector 03

Private companies

Commercial enterprises that need the same equipment, the same documentation and the same access to the manufacturer.

For private buyers the constraint is usually schedule and uptime rather than procedure. We specify against the job, quote a delivery position, and keep the spares path open.

  • Specification against the application
  • Delivery position stated up front
  • Installation and commissioning
  • Spares and service support

Accredited agency · Partners

We act for the manufacturer, in Pakistan.

Consolidated Engineers works as the accredited agent in Pakistan on behalf of foreign principals and manufacturers. That means the manufacturer's technical position reaches you through a local entity that carries the contract, and the end user has a single point of accountability for delivery and support.
