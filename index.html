After-sales services

The equipment is the beginning of the relationship, not the end of it.

A crane that arrives without commissioning, or a tow tractor without operator training, is an unfinished delivery. We treat the after-sales scope as part of the supply.

Who we are

A single accountable supplier for complex procurement.

Procurement in defence and public infrastructure rarely fails because the equipment is unavailable. It fails because a timeline slips, a specification drifts, or nobody owns the problem after the invoice is paid.

More about the firm

Consolidated Engineers exists to remove that risk. We source from established international manufacturers, hold the local accountability for import, documentation, commissioning and after-sales support, and stay on the file for the life of the equipment.
